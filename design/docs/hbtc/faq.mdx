This page answers the questions people ask most often about `hBTC`. Each
answer links to the page that has the full detail.

## What is hBTC, and is it backed 1:1?

`hBTC` is a fungible `Coin<BTC>` on Sui. Hashi mints it when you deposit BTC
and burns it when you withdraw, so every `hBTC` corresponds to BTC held in the
Hashi UTXO pool. The pool is controlled by the Hashi committee, a subset of Sui
validators using threshold signing, together with the Guardian. See
[Introduction](../README.mdx) and [Committee](../committee.mdx).

## Is there a HASHI token?

No. `hBTC` is the only coin Hashi creates. There is no governance, utility, or
airdrop token. Committee members govern the protocol by voting onchain with
their validator stake weight. Treat any HASHI token offering, presale, or
airdrop as an imitation. See [User Flows](../user-flows.mdx).

## Who controls the BTC I deposit?

Every deposit address is a Taproot address that only the Hashi committee and
the Guardian can spend together. No single party holds the key. If the
Guardian key is ever lost, a recovery script lets the committee alone spend a
UTXO 60 days after it confirms, so funds cannot be stranded. See
[Bitcoin Address Scheme](../address-scheme.mdx) and
[Guardian](../guardian.mdx).

## How long does a deposit take?

A deposit passes through 3 waits before Hashi mints `hBTC`:

1. The funding transaction must reach
   [`bitcoin_confirmation_threshold`](../config.mdx#bitcoin_confirmation_threshold)
   Bitcoin blocks, 6 by default, which is about an hour.
2. The committee approves the deposit. On Mainnet, members screen the Bitcoin
   transaction and the Sui recipient first. See
   [Handling Sanctioned Addresses](../sanctions.mdx).
3. After approval, the
   [`bitcoin_deposit_time_delay_ms`](../config.mdx#bitcoin_deposit_time_delay_ms)
   window must pass, 10 minutes by default, before anyone can confirm the
   deposit and mint.

A request that is not confirmed within 24 hours expires, and you can submit
the UTXO again. See the [Deposit](../deposit.mdx) lifecycle.

## How long does a withdrawal take?

There is no fixed time. A request waits for committee approval, then for the
leader to batch it with other approved requests, then for capacity in the
Guardian's rate limiter, and finally for the Bitcoin transaction to reach the
confirmation threshold. Most of the variation comes from the rate limiter and
from Bitcoin fee conditions. You can read the limiter's current capacity with
`client.hashi.guardian.canWithdraw` in the SDK before you submit. See
[Rate Limiting Withdrawals](../limiter.mdx) and the [Withdraw](../withdraw.mdx)
lifecycle.

## What are the minimum amounts?

Both minimums are governance parameters, 30,000 satoshis by default:

- [`bitcoin_deposit_minimum`](../config.mdx#bitcoin_deposit_minimum) for
  deposits.
- [`bitcoin_withdrawal_minimum`](../config.mdx#bitcoin_withdrawal_minimum)
  for withdrawals. This minimum exists so that every request can pay the
  worst-case miner fee and still leave an output above Bitcoin's dust
  threshold.

Read the live values with `hashi config on-chain` in the CLI or
`client.hashi.view.all()` in the SDK.

## What fees do I pay?

Hashi charges no protocol fee. Deposits are free apart from Sui gas for the
request transaction. Withdrawals pay the Bitcoin miner fee, which Hashi deducts
from your output, plus Sui gas. See [Fees](../fees.mdx).

## Why did I receive less BTC than I withdrew?

The difference is the Bitcoin miner fee for your share of the withdrawal
transaction. Hashi estimates the fee from the connected Bitcoin node, caps it,
and never charges more than the worst-case fee derived from the withdrawal
minimum. See [Withdrawal fees](../fees.mdx#withdrawal-fees).

## Why is my withdrawal still waiting?

Check these causes in order:

- **Rate limiter capacity:** The Guardian's token bucket is empty, and your
  request waits for it to refill. A request larger than the bucket's maximum
  capacity is skipped until governance raises the limit. You can
  [cancel it](cancel.mdx).
- **Protocol paused:** When the
  [`paused`](../config.mdx#paused) flag is set, queued requests stay queued
  and resume when the committee unpauses.
- **Reconfiguration:** Hashi pauses withdrawals while the committee hands the
  MPC key to the next epoch's committee. See
  [Reconfiguration](../reconfiguration.mdx).
- **Slow Bitcoin confirmation:** If the transaction is already broadcast, you
  can speed it up by spending your output with a higher-fee child. See
  [Stuck transactions](../fees.mdx#stuck-transactions).

## Which Bitcoin addresses can I withdraw to?

`P2WPKH` (bech32) and `P2TR` (bech32m) addresses on the Bitcoin network that
matches your Sui network. Hashi does not support `P2WSH` or legacy address
types. See [Withdraw BTC](withdraw.mdx#before-you-withdraw).

## Can I deposit the same UTXO twice?

No. Hashi records each UTXO it accepts, and a request for a UTXO that is
already in the pool aborts onchain. In the SDK, check with
`client.hashi.view.findUsedUtxos` before you submit. See
[Deposit BTC](deposit.mdx#deposit-with-the-typescript-sdk).

## I sent BTC but did not record the deposit. Is it lost?

No. The BTC sits at your deposit address until you record it. Hashi mints
`hBTC` only after you submit the deposit request on Sui, so submit it when you
are ready. See [Deposit BTC](deposit.mdx).

## Can a deposit be rejected?

Yes, in 3 cases: the amount is below the minimum, the request expires before
the committee confirms it, or a quorum of committee members declines to
approve it after screening. A deposit that is below the minimum is rejected
when you submit the request. See [Handling Sanctioned Addresses](../sanctions.mdx).

## Where can I try Hashi without real BTC?

On Testnet, which pairs Sui Testnet with Bitcoin Signet. Signet coins have no
value and come from public faucets. See
[Try a deposit on Testnet](deposit.mdx#try-a-deposit-on-testnet).
