Module guardian 

Source
Expand description

What a guardian reports about itself: through a slot’s tunnel, through the proxy’s relay, and on the proxy’s public /info. gRPC answers are decoded by grpcurl with the guardian commit’s own protos, so this tool never reads a guardian with another build’s field or enum numbering.

The proxy’s public /info, which describes the guardian it fronts. Its u64s are strings.
Reached
A slot’s guardian, as reached through its tunnel and as the proxy leads to it.

Constants§

CURRENT_FOR
How old /info can be and still show that the proxy reaches its guardian: the proxy caches it for 30 seconds by default, and serves its last view, however old, while a refresh is in flight or failing.
