Timing bounds of the withdrawal and deposit flows. Timing bounds of the withdrawal and deposit flows.

Why it exists: the bounds describe the protocol, not a deployment. The guardian signs within minutes of a Hashi approval, a Bitcoin block time may run hours off real time, and six confirmations take about an hour. Every environment runs the same values, so they live here and not in the config file.

How to change a bound: edit the constant and its reason below. If an environment ever needs a different set, select it on deployment.retention_environment and keep both sets in this file.

The bounds, with E1, E2 and E3 as in domain:

  • Withdrawals: each successor must follow its predecessor within its delay. Each predecessor may be recorded after its successor by at most its skew.
  • Deposits: the deposit’s block time may be after the Hashi approval by at most the skew. The Bitcoin event is the user’s payment, so no delay bounds it.
  • Deposit requests: Bitcoin is asked about a request’s transaction for the watch period. The monitor assumes an honest deposit confirms on Bitcoin within it.
  • The UTXO pool: a UTXO the bridge holds is reported by its age on Bitcoin, and by its absence from Bitcoin after a grace period.

A Bitcoin event is timed by the block that gave the transaction Hashi’s confirmation threshold (see rpc::btc), not by the block that holds it.

Constants§

DEPOSIT_APPROVAL_SKEW
A deposit’s Bitcoin block after its Sui DepositApproved. A miner may date a block up to two hours ahead of real time.
DEPOSIT_REQUEST_WATCH
How long Bitcoin is asked about a deposit request’s transaction. Assumption: an honest deposit confirms on Bitcoin within two days of its request. Move marks a request expired after a day (MAX_DEPOSIT_REQUEST_AGE_MS in deposit_queue.move) and an honest leader deletes it then, so the user requests again, which is a new watch. But Move does not refuse to approve an old request. A UTXO that confirms after the watch never enters the UTXO pool, so an approval of it is reported as DepositUnbacked.
UTXO_MAX_AGE
Age of an unspent bridge UTXO that is a finding. The committee can spend it alone at 60 days, so the finding leaves 20 days to act. Coin selection in the hashi crate spends UTXOs older than 30 days first (RECOVERY_PRIORITY_AGE_BLOCKS), but only as extra inputs of a withdrawal.
WITHDRAWAL_E1_AFTER_E2_SKEW
E1 after E2. The guardian’s request clock tolerance is five minutes.
WITHDRAWAL_E1_TO_E2_DELAY
E1 -> E2. The guardian signs about 15 minutes after a Hashi approval on testnet. An hour covers a guardian restart or a reconfiguration.
WITHDRAWAL_E2_AFTER_E3_SKEW
E2 after E3. A block time is set by its miner and need only exceed the median of the last 11 blocks, which lags real time by about an hour.
WITHDRAWAL_E2_TO_E3_DELAY
E2 -> E3. The signed transaction must confirm on Bitcoin within a day. E3 is timed by the block that gave it the confirmation threshold, so the bound includes the wait for those blocks, about 50 minutes at six.
