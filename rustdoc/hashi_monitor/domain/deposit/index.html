SuiDepositApprovedEvent
The monitor’s view of DepositApproved on Sui: the committee’s claim that a deposit is paid. Move mints only after an approval and its time delay, so judging the approval can alert before the mint. Move copies the UTXO from the request, so it names the request’s outpoint, amount and path.
SuiDepositRequestedEvent
The monitor’s view of DepositRequested on Sui: the user’s claim that a Bitcoin output pays the bridge. The public deposit entry emits it, so the committee cannot suppress it. Its timestamp is the request’s own.
