The events the monitor follows, their ids, and the cursors of its sources. The monitor’s core types: the events it follows, their ids, and the cursors of its sources.

Why it exists: every source, Sui, the guardian’s S3 logs and Bitcoin, is parsed into one of these events, and the state machines reason about the events alone. Each event type has a constructor named for its source, so the mapping from a source’s shape to the monitor’s lives next to the type.

The flows:

  • A withdrawal is three events: E1, the Hashi approval on Sui; E2, the guardian’s signature in its log; E3, the transaction confirmed on Bitcoin. Every event states the transaction in Move’s shape, WithdrawalTx, so the sources can be compared as a whole.
  • A deposit is three events: E1, the user’s DepositRequested on Sui; E2, the output confirmed on Bitcoin; E3, the DepositApproved on Sui, after which Move mints. Each request is followed on its own.

Formatting of times lives in time, and the bridge keys in keys.
